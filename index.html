How to play

  • Putt: press anywhere on the hole and pull back, like a catapult. The further you pull, the harder you hit. Let go to putt. Slide back to the start to cancel.
  • The guide: dots show where the ball will go, up to its first bounce.
  • Par is the number of strokes a good shot should take. Under par is a birdie, one shot is a hole in one.
  • Hazards: sand slows the ball, ice keeps it sliding, hills pull it sideways, and water costs a stroke and sends you back.
  • Friends: add up to four players in the clubhouse. Everyone takes one shot at a time, passing the phone.
  • Helper: turn it on in Settings for a longer guide and one free do-over on every hole.
  • Daily Hole: a new hole every day, the same for the whole family. Fewest strokes wins.
