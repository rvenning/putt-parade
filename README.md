# ⛳ Putt Parade

Mini-golf for the whole family. Four courses, 36 holes, up to four players
passing one phone, a Daily Hole, and three complete art styles to switch
between — Toy Diorama, Paper Cut and Glow Night.

**Play it:** https://rvenning.github.io/putt-parade/

## Features

- **Drag back and let go.** Press anywhere on the hole, pull back like a
  catapult and release. Dots show the ball's real path up to its first bounce,
  simulated with the game's own physics.
- **Four courses × nine holes**, each introducing something new:
  - *Meadow Lane* — rails, blocks, sand, a hill, a turnstile and a windmill.
  - *Pebble Pier* — water hazards (a stroke and back you go), buoys, ferries.
  - *Funfair Fields* — speed pads, magic hats that teleport the ball, a carousel.
  - *Frosty Peaks* — ice, steep hills, waddling penguins and a snow mill.
- **Pass-the-phone play for 1–4.** Everyone takes one shot at a time, with their
  own ball colour; the others' balls stay on the green as ghosts. Guests can
  join without a profile (their scores aren't saved).
- **A real scorecard** between holes — par, birdies, aces circled — and a
  results screen with standings and course stars.
- **Helper**, per player: a longer aiming guide (two bounces) and one free
  do-over on every hole. Rounds played with the Helper are tagged on the
  leaderboard.
- **Daily Hole** — one hole from the 36, picked by the date and mirrored on some
  days, the same for the whole family. Fewest strokes wins today.
- **Three art styles**, chosen in Settings and remembered per device. Each
  changes everything: the course art, the surround, the ball, the HUD, the
  menus, the scorecard and the music.
- **Family leaderboard** per course, for the daily hole and for aces.
- Synthesised sound effects and a little tune per style (toy marimba, music
  box, synth arps), with separate toggles. Respects reduced motion.

## Scoring

Par for every hole comes from bots playing the real engine (`tools/par.js`):
the mean of the adult and eight-year-old bots plus 0.6, rounded. Strokes are
capped at par + 4 (the ball is picked up). A round earns one star for
finishing, two and three for beating each course's bands; finishing a course
in any score opens the next.

## Built on gamekit

Profiles, PINs, family sync, the leaderboard, sounds, PWA install and the
version line come from [gamekit](https://github.com/rvenning/gamekit),
vendored into `lib/`. To update it:

```
node ../gamekit/tools/sync-to-game.js .
node lib/tools/stamp-version.js . --bump
```

## How it is put together

- `js/physics.js` — a ball on a flat green: rails, pegs, bumpers, spinners,
  sliders, portals, sand, ice, slopes and pads. Fixed 1/240 s step, no
  `Math.random`, everything moving is a pure function of the hole clock.
- `js/hole.js` — compiles a hole (rail grid, regions, distance-to-cup field).
- `js/courses.js` — the 36 holes as data on a fixed 400×700 field.
- `js/game.js` — `HoleSim` (one hole, turns, penalties, do-overs) and `Match`
  (a round and its scorecard). DOM-free.
- `js/bot.js` — one shot planner for the title-screen demos, the par
  calibration and the tests, with skill expressed as aim and power wobble.
- `js/art.js` + `js/style-*.js` — the three art styles behind one interface.
- `js/render.js`, `js/input.js`, `js/main.js` — camera, aiming, app shell.

## Tests

```
npm test
```

Lints every hole (inside the rails, cup reachable, nothing can squeeze a ball
against a rail, a ball left anywhere settles somewhere playable, hard shots
never tunnel), checks the save merge both ways round, the turn rules, and plays
every course with bots — a five-year-old-level bot must never get stuck and an
adult bot must beat par.

## PWA files

`manifest.json`, `sw.js` (network-first; bump with `stamp-version.js --bump`),
`icons/` (generated by `node tools/make-icons.js`), `version.json`.

## Local development

```
npx http-server . -p 8139 -c-1
```

`?debug=1` opens the gamekit debug panel (saving is switched off there).

## Storage

localStorage keys are prefixed `putt_`; family sync uses the `puttparade`
collection in the shared Firebase project. The Firebase API key in
`js/firebase-config.js` is a public client key restricted to Firestore; the
rules require an anonymous sign-in.
